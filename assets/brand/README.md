# NuvelloWeb Brand Assets

## Concept

The open brick-red corners signal an adaptable framework. The blue-teal “N” carries the established Nuvello color language, while the brick-red wave represents momentum, connection, and the “NuWave” idea without turning the mark into a literal illustration.

## Primary use

- Use `nuvelloweb-logo-dark.svg` on charcoal, navy, photography, or other dark surfaces.
- Use `nuvelloweb-logo-light.svg` on white and light neutral surfaces.
- Use `nuvelloweb-mark.svg` when the full wordmark will be too small or space is square.
- Use monochrome variants only when color reproduction is unavailable or a single-color treatment is required.

## Clear space and sizing

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the height of the “N.”
- Do not use the horizontal lockup below 180 pixels wide on screen.
- Below that size, switch to the standalone mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, shadow, or rearrange the elements.
