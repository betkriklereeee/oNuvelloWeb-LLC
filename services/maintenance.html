Website care plans

Your site should keep working after launch.

Updates, backups, security, performance checks, and practical support when your business needs a change.

The approach

A website is an operating asset. Care plans keep the core software healthy, catch issues sooner, and give you a reliable place to send the updates that do not justify a new project.

Every engagement starts with the business context and the customer experience. From there, we define the smallest complete solution that can do the job well and support what comes next.

  • Clear deliverables and decision points
  • Responsive, accessible, and performance-minded
  • Ownership and handoff defined upfront
  • Direct communication throughout

Process

A clear path from problem to progress.

The exact scope changes. The accountability does not.

Baseline review

Document the site, hosting, access, dependencies, and known issues.

Stabilize

Create backups, apply safe updates, and address immediate health concerns.

Monitor

Watch the agreed health signals and recurring maintenance schedule.

Update

Handle routine platform and content changes within the plan.

Report

Summarize completed work and issues that need a decision.

Improve

Recommend focused enhancements when the data or business priorities support them.
