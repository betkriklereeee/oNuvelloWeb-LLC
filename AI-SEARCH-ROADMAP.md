# NuvelloWeb AI Search & Organic Growth Roadmap

## Objective

Earn qualified Los Angeles web-design and digital-marketing inquiries by becoming an unusually clear, credible, and citable source. Rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed; the program measures visibility, qualified traffic, citations, calls, and submitted leads.

## Publishing standard

Each page must answer the core question in the opening section, use a descriptive title and URL, link to the most relevant service page, include original examples, and end with a useful next step. Add FAQ markup only when the questions and answers are visibly present. Never fabricate reviews, performance data, certifications, clients, locations, or rankings.

## Entity and authority work

1. Complete and verify the Google Business Profile with the exact NuvelloWeb name, Los Angeles service area, phone, domain, categories, hours, services, and original photos.
2. Keep the same business name, phone, email, and location across LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, reputable directories, and industry profiles.
3. Request genuine client reviews after successful milestones; reply to every review without inserting unnatural keywords.
4. Build one detailed case study at a time with permission, problem, constraints, approach, delivered work, and verified outcome.
5. Earn relevant links through partner profiles, vendor directories, local organizations, guest expertise, and genuinely useful resources.

## Measurement setup

- Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after the production files are deployed.
- Submit `https://nuvelloweb.com/sitemap.xml` to both platforms.
- Use the included IndexNow key to notify participating engines after production changes.
- Connect the site's built-in `generate_lead`, `phone_click`, `email_click`, and form-status events to the chosen analytics platform.
- Review monthly: non-branded impressions, local queries, qualified organic visits, AI/referral traffic, calls, form leads, indexed pages, cited pages, and conversion rate.
- Update sitemap modification dates only when a page's meaningful content changes.

## 90-day sequence

### Month 1 — establish trust

Deploy the technical discovery layer, connect webmaster tools, finish the business profile, publish the Los Angeles web-design page, and collect source material for the first case study.

### Month 2 — answer buyer questions

Publish the cost, timeline, redesign, and lead-diagnosis pages. Add original examples and strengthen internal links based on early query data.

### Month 3 — deepen topical authority

Publish the platform-comparison and maintenance pages plus the first approved case study. Review search and AI referrals, then prioritize the next pages from actual demand rather than producing content at volume.
